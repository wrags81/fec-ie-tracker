# What changed in the last four weeks

Data through 2026-10-01, pulled 2026-10-02. "Last four weeks" means the weeks starting 9/7, 9/14, 9/21 and 9/28 (the last is partial), compared with the four weeks starting 8/10 to 8/31. Figures are general-election independent expenditures by the tracked committees only.

## Volume

- Republican-side committees reported $406.7 million in the last four weeks, up from $53.0 million in the prior four. Democratic-side committees reported $138.7 million, up from $22.3 million.
- House spending went from $3.3 million to $93.9 million on the Republican side and from $0.5 million to $45.1 million on the Democratic side.
- The Republican side spent in 57 House races in the last four weeks (16 in the prior four); the Democratic side spent in 37 (6 in the prior four).
- No Going Back PAC first reported spending on 9/2 and accounts for $126.7 million of the Republican-side total in the last four weeks, across 64 races.
- Texas PAC first reported spending the week of 9/7 and accounts for $131.5 million, all in the Texas Senate race: $60.5 million the week of 9/7 and $61.4 million the week of 9/28.

## Senate share shifts

- Ohio rose most on both sides. SLF's Ohio share went from 9.0% to 28.8% ($3.6 million to $28.0 million). WinSenate's went from 15.1% to 30.4% ($3.3 million to $28.5 million). No Going Back PAC put $22.6 million into Ohio after none in the prior window.
- North Carolina fell most as a share on both sides while rising in dollars. WinSenate's North Carolina share went from 38.8% to 12.5% ($8.4 million to $11.7 million). SLF's went from 26.9% to 14.0% ($10.6 million to $13.6 million).
- WinSenate entered New Hampshire the week of 9/7 and spent $11.3 million there in the last four weeks (12.0% of its total). SLF entered the same week ($4.6 million).
- SLF's Georgia share rose from 2.9% to 8.6% ($1.1 million to $8.4 million). Its Michigan and Maine shares fell (24.0% to 16.8% and 16.4% to 9.7%) while dollars rose in both.
- Texas rose from 18.9% to 36.2% of Republican-side spending ($10.0 million to $147.1 million), almost all of it Texas PAC. Every other Senate race fell as a share of the Republican-side total except Ohio (6.7% to 12.4%), New Hampshire, Georgia and Florida. No Going Back PAC reported no Texas Senate spending in the last two weeks after $5.6 million in the two weeks before.

## House

- CLF, HMP and No Going Back PAC had little or no general-election spending before 8/31, so their four-week share changes compare against a very small base and are not meaningful. The entries below are the clearer signal.
- CLF entered 25 races between the weeks of 8/31 and 9/21; the most recent are MI-10, OH-01, PA-07 and WI-03 (week of 9/21).
- HMP entered 37 races between the weeks of 8/31 and 9/28; the most recent are FL-13, FL-14 and FL-22 (week of 9/28) and AZ-01, FL-25, ME-02 and NM-02 (week of 9/21).
- The largest House totals in the last four weeks were FL-25 ($8.2 million, $7.4 million of it Republican-side), NY-03 ($6.6 million, all Republican-side), TX-34 ($6.1 million) and CO-08 ($6.1 million).
- Safety & Affordability PAC first reported spending the week of 9/28: $1.3 million across AZ-01, PA-07 and WI-03.

## Stopped spending

- Only two committee-race pairs show two or more full weeks of silence after earlier activity: SLF in NE-SEN (one $100,000 expenditure the week of 6/1) and WinSenate in TX-SEN (one $50,000 expenditure the week of 8/31).
